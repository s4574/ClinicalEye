## pancreas_head

**Aliases:** pancreatic head, head of pancreas, uncinate process, pancreas head and uncinate process

**Definition:** Enlarged right part of the pancreas lying within the C-loop of the duodenum; the uncinate process is the part of the lower head derived from the ventral bud.

**Vertebral level:** Pancreas as a whole crosses L1-L2.

**Relations:**
- posterior: inferior vena cava; renal vein; common bile duct (groove on posterosuperior surface of head)
- left: pancreatic neck / body (pancreas continues to the left toward the spleen)
- right: duodenum (C-loop surrounds the head; 2nd part)
- other: uncinate process lies posterior to the SMA; uncinate process crosses anterior to the aorta between SMA and IMA origins (with left renal vein and 3rd part of duodenum)

**Axial CT identification:** Soft-tissue gland on slices around L1-L2, nestled in the concavity of the duodenal C-loop (2nd part lateral/right), lying on (anterior to) the IVC; the uncinate process is the lower head part extending behind the SMA, anterior to the aorta. The distal CBD runs in a groove on its posterosuperior surface toward the 2nd part of the duodenum.

**Localize from segmented masks:** Take the right-most part of `pancreas` that is enclosed by `duodenum` (C-loop) and lies directly anterior to `inferior_vena_cava`; the uncinate is the lowest/most medial head tissue lying anterior to `aorta`.

**Variants (not typical anatomy):**
- Pancreas divisum (most common developmental anomaly, ~10-15%): failure of bud fusion (ductal variant).
- Annular pancreas: pancreatic tissue encircling the duodenum from incomplete rotation of the ventral bud (variant).
- Circumportal pancreas: pancreatic tissue encircling the portal vein/its tributaries, thought to arise when the uncinate process fuses with the body (variant).

## pancreatic_neck

**Aliases:** pancreas neck, neck of pancreas

**Definition:** Short segment of the pancreas joining head and body; lies in front of the portal venous (splenoportal) confluence.

**Vertebral level:** Pancreas crosses L1-L2.

**Relations:**
- anterior: pylorus of the stomach
- posterior: portal venous confluence (SMV + splenic vein forming the portal vein); SMV passes posterior to the neck; SMA (neck is an anterior relation of the SMA)
- left: pancreatic body
- right: pancreatic head

**Axial CT identification:** The narrow pancreatic segment directly anterior to the junction of the SMV and splenic vein (portal confluence), with the pylorus in front of it; the SMA lies further posterior.

**Localize from segmented masks:** Within `pancreas`, find the slice/position directly anterior to the confluence region of `portal_vein_and_splenic_vein` (where the splenic vein meets the portal vein); tissue to the right of that point is head, to the left is body.

## pancreatic_body

**Aliases:** pancreas body, body of pancreas

**Definition:** Part of the pancreas continuing left from the neck, crossing anterior to the aorta and L2.

**Vertebral level:** Passes over L2 (pancreas overall L1-L2).

**Relations:**
- anterior: stomach (body lies posterior to the stomach)
- posterior: aorta; SMA; left adrenal gland; left kidney; renal vessels; splenic vein (passes posterior to the pancreas)
- left: pancreatic tail
- right: pancreatic neck
- superior: splenic artery (runs along the superior pancreas)
- inferior: root of transverse mesocolon along inferior border of pancreas
- other: between celiac and SMA origins, the splenic vein and body of pancreas cross anterior to the aorta

**Axial CT identification:** Gland crossing anterior to the aorta/SMA at about L2, posterior to the stomach, with the splenic vein running along its posterior surface and the splenic artery along its superior border.

**Localize from segmented masks:** Portion of `pancreas` lying directly anterior to `aorta`, posterior to `stomach`, and anterior to `kidney_left`/`adrenal_gland_left`; the splenic-vein part of `portal_vein_and_splenic_vein` runs along its posterior surface.

**Variants (not typical anatomy):**
- Dorsal pancreatic agenesis (partial or complete; absence of body and tail) - variant, often asymptomatic.

## pancreatic_tail

**Aliases:** pancreas tail, tail of pancreas

**Definition:** Left-most part of the pancreas, extending toward the splenic hilum; the only part not retroperitoneal (lies in the splenorenal ligament).

**Relations:**
- posterior: left kidney (tail lies anterior to it; may cover the renal hilum)
- left: splenic hilum
- right: pancreatic body
- other: closely related to the splenic hilum and the left colic (splenic) flexure; contained in the splenorenal ligament; stomach lies anterior (pancreas is a posterior relation of the stomach)

**Axial CT identification:** Tapering left end of the gland, anterior to the left kidney, reaching toward the splenic hilum, near the splenic flexure of the colon.

**Localize from segmented masks:** Left-most part of `pancreas`, anterior to `kidney_left` and pointing to the medial (hilar) surface of `spleen`; `colon` (splenic flexure) is nearby.

## superior_mesenteric_artery

**Aliases:** SMA, superior mesenteric artery

**Definition:** Second major (unpaired, ventral) branch of the abdominal aorta, supplying the midgut.

**Vertebral level:** Origin at L1, about 1 cm below the celiac trunk and above the renal arteries.

**Relations:**
- anterior: pylorus of the stomach; neck of the pancreas; splenic vein; transverse colon
- posterior: uncinate process of the pancreas; inferior (3rd) portion of the duodenum; left renal vein (between SMA and aorta); aorta
- right: SMV (lies to the right of the SMA)
- superior: celiac trunk origin (~1 cm above)
- inferior: renal artery origins (just below SMA origin)
- other: posterior surface of the pancreatic body is in contact with the SMA

**Axial CT identification:** Round arterial cross-section arising from the anterior aorta at L1 and descending anterior to it; the left renal vein crosses between SMA and aorta, the 3rd part of the duodenum and uncinate process lie behind it, the SMV lies to its right, and the splenic vein/pancreatic neck lie in front.

**Localize from segmented masks:** Start at the anterior wall of `aorta` about 1 cm caudal to the celiac origin (at L1, `vertebrae_L1`); follow the vessel caudally anterior to `aorta`, posterior to the neck of `pancreas` and to the splenic-vein part of `portal_vein_and_splenic_vein`, with the 3rd part of `duodenum` passing behind it.

**Variants (not typical anatomy):**
- Celiacomesenteric trunk: celiac trunk and SMA share a common origin (variant).
- Replaced/aberrant right hepatic artery arising from the SMA (~20%) (variant).
- Right gastric and right hepatic arteries may arise from the SMA (variant).

## superior_mesenteric_vein

**Aliases:** SMV, superior mesenteric vein

**Definition:** Main venous drainage of the jejunum and ileum; ascends to the right of the SMA and joins the splenic vein behind the pancreatic neck to form the portal vein.

**Relations:**
- anterior: neck of the pancreas (SMV passes posterior to it)
- posterior: inferior pancreaticoduodenal artery (courses to the right posterior to the SMV)
- left: SMA
- superior: joins splenic vein -> portal vein
- other: 3rd part of duodenum lies posterior to the superior mesenteric vessels

**Axial CT identification:** Contrast-filled vein just to the right of the SMA, followed cranially until it passes behind the pancreatic neck and merges with the splenic vein.

**Localize from segmented masks:** Not a separate TotalSegmentator class (it may be partly included at the inferior end of `portal_vein_and_splenic_vein`); look immediately to the right of the SMA, anterior to the 3rd part of `duodenum`, caudal to the neck of `pancreas`.

**Variants (not typical anatomy):**
- SMV formed by two trunks draining directly into the splenoportal confluence (common variant); the left trunk frequently runs ventral to the SMA.
- Single main trunk of variable length (5-50 mm) before dividing into right and left intestinal branches.

## portal_vein

**Aliases:** portal vein, main portal vein, hepatic portal vein, splenoportal confluence, portal confluence, portal venous confluence

**Definition:** Vein formed behind the pancreatic neck by the union of the SMV and splenic vein (splenoportal confluence); ascends in the hepatoduodenal ligament and divides at the liver hilum into right and left branches.

**Relations:**
- anterior: neck of pancreas (at the confluence); proper hepatic artery and common bile duct (in the hepatoduodenal ligament)
- posterior: inferior vena cava (posterior border of the epiploic foramen)
- superior: liver hilum (porta hepatis), where it bifurcates
- inferior: SMV
- other: most posterior structure within the hepatoduodenal ligament; porta hepatis: CBD lateral, hepatic artery medial, portal vein posterior; caudate lobe (segment I) lies between portal vein and IVC

**Axial CT identification:** Large vein running obliquely up and to the right from behind the pancreatic neck to the porta hepatis; in the hepatoduodenal ligament it lies posterior to the CBD and hepatic artery, and anterior to the IVC. At the hilum it divides into right (to V/VIII and VI/VII) and left branches; the left has a transverse then an umbilical portion toward the umbilical fissure.

**Localize from segmented masks:** `portal_vein_and_splenic_vein` (portal part): follow from the confluence behind the neck of `pancreas` cranially and rightward to the liver hilum; it lies anterior to `inferior_vena_cava` and caudal/anterior to `liver_segment_1`; the bifurcation level marks the transverse plane between superior (II, IVa, VII, VIII) and inferior (III, IVb, V, VI) segments.

**Variants (not typical anatomy):**
- Branching variants in up to 35%: trifurcation of the main portal vein; right posterior branch arising from the main portal vein; right anterior branch arising from the left portal vein (variants).
- Duplication and non-branching portal vein are rare variants; agenesis of the left or right portal vein is the most common congenital anomaly.

## inferior_vena_cava

## kidneys

**Definition:** Paired retroperitoneal bean-shaped organs between T12 and L3, with a medial hilum (renal vein anterior, artery, pelvis posterior).
